Day Tradr is a watchlist for the symbols you actually follow, with the numbers laid out so you can read them at a glance.

The list opens on a summary of the day: how many of your symbols are up, how many are down, and the best and worst movers by name. Every symbol below gets a card — its name, a sparkline of recent movement, the price, and a change chip you can read from across the room. Sort by your own order, by name, or by today's move, and switch every chip between the change, the percentage, or both.

Tap a symbol for the full picture: a price chart with 20- and 50-day moving averages you can turn off, a volume strip beneath it, and range bars showing where the price sits inside today's high and low and inside the year's. Five ranges, from a month to everything the source has.

WHAT YOU GET

• Stocks, ETFs, commodity futures and currency pairs, added by ticker or picked from suggestions
• A summary of the day's movers above the list, not buried inside it
• Moving-average overlays and range bars on the detail chart
• A watchlist you can reorder by dragging, and edit by swiping a row away
• English, French, Arabic and Simplified Chinese, with right-to-left layout in Arabic
• Light and dark, following your system or set by hand

END-OF-DAY QUOTES

Data comes from Yahoo Finance's free end-of-day quotes. This is not a real-time feed and Day Tradr is not a trading app: there is no account, no brokerage connection, and nothing to buy or sell here.
