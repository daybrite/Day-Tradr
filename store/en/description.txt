Day Tradr is a watchlist for the symbols you actually follow, with the numbers laid out so you can read them at a glance.

Every symbol gets a card: its name, a sparkline of recent movement, the price, and a change chip you can read from across the room. Tap one for the full picture — a price chart with a volume strip, and the statistics that matter: open, high, low, volume, 52-week high and low, previous close, a 20-day moving average, and the number of sessions behind it.

WHAT YOU GET

• Stocks, commodities and currency pairs, added by ticker or picked from suggestions
• Sparklines on the list, full charts on the detail screen
• A statistics grid that does not make you scroll to find the number you wanted
• English, French, Arabic and Simplified Chinese, with right-to-left layout in Arabic
• Light and dark, following your system or set by hand

END-OF-DAY QUOTES

Data comes from Yahoo Finance's free end-of-day quotes. This is not a real-time feed and Day Tradr is not a trading app: there is no account, no brokerage connection, and nothing to buy or sell here.

Open source, built with the Day framework — one Rust codebase drawing itself with each platform's real native widgets. github.com/daybrite/Day-Tradr
